Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publishing]

Vale is a desktop app for Windows 11. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can delete it. In short: the app has no server, collects no analytics, and keeps your data on your own PC.

Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, the app asks Google for these permissions:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
calendar.calendarlist.readonlyReads the list of your calendars, so their events and colours can be shown.
calendar.eventsReads your events to show them on the Calendar page; creates or updates an event only when you press Save in the event editor. The app never deletes events.
  • Storage. Your sign-in token is stored on your PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI so only your Windows account can read it. The most recent calendar data is cached on your PC so the calendar works offline.
  • Sharing. Google user data is sent only between your PC and Google. It is not sent to the developer, to any server of ours, or to any third party, and it is not sold.
  • Use. Google user data is used only to show and edit your calendar inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
Vale's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data on your PC

Everything else the app keeps lives in %APPDATA%\Vale\ on your PC: settings, notes, reminders, CLI history and cached lyrics. None of it is uploaded.

  • Notifications and media. The app reads Windows notifications and the currently playing track to show them in the island. This stays on your PC.
  • Lyrics (off by default). If you turn on lyrics, the artist, title and length of the playing track are sent to lrclib.net to fetch synced lyrics.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings → Integrations. This deletes the stored token.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the folder %APPDATA%\Vale\ to remove all local data, or uninstall with the uninstall script and DI_PURGE=1.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Material changes are also noted in the release notes on GitHub.
