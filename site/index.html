Calendar, pomodoro, reminders, notes, music and a small terminal, one hover away. A native Windows 11 app, free and open source.

Everything you check ten times a day

Hover the notch to open the panel, scroll to change pages. When nothing is happening, it is just a clock.

Calendar

Your Google Calendar by day, week or month. Add and edit events without opening a browser.

Pomodoro

A focus timer with a ring in the notch, so you always know how long is left.

Reminders

Once, daily or weekly. Missed ones show up when you are back.

Music

Cover, controls and optional synced lyrics for whatever is playing.

CLI

Run a quick command in PowerShell or cmd, right inside the panel.

The cat

A small cat in the notch whose face and ears tell you what is going on.

How Vale uses Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you do, this is exactly what happens.

  • 1
    You sign in with GoogleIn the browser, on Google's own page. Vale never sees your password.
  • 2
    It reads your calendar list and eventsTo show them on the Calendar page, by day, week or month.
  • 3
    It adds or edits events you ask it toOnly when you press Save in the event editor. It never deletes events.
  • 4
    Everything stays on your PCThere is no Vale server. Data goes only between your PC and Google.

Permissions requested

calendar.calendarlist.readonly
See the list of your calendars.

calendar.events
View and edit events on your calendars.

You can disconnect any time in Settings → Integrations, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Details in the privacy policy.
